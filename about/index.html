About us

Meet the builder behind Quality Building Services.

Quality Building Services is a Canberra residential builder based in Mitchell, led by director Brad Freer. Brad completed his carpentry apprenticeship in 1993 and, after a decade sub-contracting to local builders, founded Quality Building Services — bringing more than 25 years of construction, renovation, extension and carpentry experience across Canberra and NSW.

Brad is a hands-on builder who takes the time to understand each client’s questions and design a solution that suits how they live. Because he knows clients lead busy lives, he makes himself available after hours for project updates and queries.
